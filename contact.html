Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign in to CRM Lite?

Open the CRM Lite app on your Android device and select "Sign in with Google." Follow the prompts to authenticate with your Google account. Once signed in, you can start creating and managing your customer records.

Where is my data stored?

All your customer data, transaction history, and business records are stored exclusively on your mobile device. We do not store your data on servers or in the cloud. Your data remains under your complete control on your phone.

Is my data secure?

Yes. CRM Lite uses Google Sign-In for secure authentication, and all customer data is stored locally on your device. We recommend protecting your phone with a PIN, password, or biometric security (fingerprint/face recognition) to ensure your data remains secure.

Can I use CRM Lite without internet?

Yes! Since all data is stored on your device, CRM Lite works offline. You can create customers, search records, and manage transactions without needing an internet connection. Internet is only required for initial Google Sign-In authentication.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Your customer data is stored on your device's storage. If you uninstall the app, your data will be deleted. We recommend backing up important data before uninstalling. You can reinstall CRM Lite and sign in again to start fresh.

Can I export my customer data?

Currently, CRM Lite does not support exporting customer data. Data is stored locally on your device. If you need to transfer data, please contact us at dk22222233@gmail.com.

How do I delete a customer record?

Open the customer record in the app, look for the delete or remove option, and confirm the deletion. Deleted records cannot be recovered, so make sure you want to remove the customer before confirming.

How do I change my password?

CRM Lite uses Google Sign-In, so there's no separate password to manage. To change your Google account password, visit Google Account and update your password there.

What do I do if I forget my customer's information?

All customer records are stored in CRM Lite. Use the search feature to find customers by name or phone number. If you've deleted a customer record, unfortunately it cannot be recovered. Please keep regular backups of important customer data.

How do I request data deletion?

To delete your account and all associated data, please visit the Data Deletion Request page for complete instructions. You can also email dk22222233@gmail.com to request deletion.

Is CRM Lite free?

Yes, CRM Lite is completely free to download and use. No hidden fees, no premium tiers. or "CRM Lite offers a free version with basic features..."

What information does Google provide to CRM Lite?

When you sign in with Google, we receive: your name, email address, profile picture, and unique Google ID. This information is used only for authentication and to personalize your app experience. We do not collect or share additional information from your Google account.

How to Get the Best Support

To help us assist you quickly and effectively, please include the following information in your email:

  • Subject: A clear, brief description of your issue (e.g., "Cannot add customer" or "Sign-in problem")
  • Device Info: Your phone model and Android version (e.g., Samsung Galaxy A12, Android 11)
  • App Version: The version of CRM Lite you're using (check in Settings > About)
  • Description: A detailed explanation of what happened and what you were trying to do
  • Steps to Reproduce: How we can recreate the issue (if applicable)
  • Screenshots: If relevant, attach screenshots showing the issue

The more details you provide, the faster we can help!
