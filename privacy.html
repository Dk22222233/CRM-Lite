Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Introduction

CRM Lite ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, process, and protect your information when you use the CRM Lite mobile application.

This policy applies to all users of CRM Lite. Please read this policy carefully. By using CRM Lite, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information from Google Sign-In

CRM Lite uses Google Sign-In for secure authentication. When you sign in with your Google account, we receive the following information from Google:

  • Your full name as associated with your Google account
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile picture (if available)
  • Your unique Google account identifier (Google ID)

These details are provided by Google's authentication system and are only used to authenticate your identity and personalize your app experience.

2.2 Customer Data and Business Records

CRM Lite allows you to create and manage customer records within the app. This information is entered directly by you and includes:

  • Customer names and contact information
  • Transaction history and payment records
  • Outstanding amounts owed
  • Any notes or details you add about your customers

You have full control over what customer data you enter into the app.

2.3 Usage and Diagnostic Data

We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic data. CRM Lite stores all information exclusively on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Google Account Information

The information received from Google Sign-In is used for:

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and grant you secure access to your account
  • Personalization: To display your name and profile picture in the app
  • Account Management: To manage your user account and preferences

We do not use your Google information for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose beyond app authentication and functionality.

3.2 Customer Data

Your customer data and business records are used exclusively by you within the app. These records are stored locally on your device and are not processed, analyzed, or used for any other purpose.

3.3 Business Operations

[OPTIONAL: We may use anonymized, non-identifiable information to improve app performance and functionality. Customize or remove this if not applicable.]

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local-Only Storage

CRM Lite is designed with privacy as a core principle:

  • All customer data is stored exclusively on your mobile device. No data is sent to our servers or stored in the cloud.
  • Your business records, customer information, and transaction history remain under your complete control on your phone.
  • We do not maintain backups of your customer data on external servers.

4.2 Security Measures

We implement security best practices to protect your data:

  • Google Sign-In uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 protocol for secure authentication
  • Your Google authentication credentials are never stored by the app; Google handles authentication securely
  • Local data storage utilizes your device's built-in security features
  • We encourage you to keep your phone protected with a PIN, password, or biometric security

4.3 Limitations

While we take security seriously, no system is completely secure. Since your data is stored locally on your device:

  • If your phone is lost or stolen, data may be at risk unless your device is password-protected
  • We recommend backing up important data and keeping your device secure
  • CRM Lite cannot recover deleted data if it is permanently removed from your device

5. Sharing of Information

CRM Lite does not share, sell, or disclose your personal information or business data to third parties.

  • Your customer data remains entirely on your device and is never shared
  • Your Google account information is used only for authentication and is not shared with anyone
  • We do not sell your data to advertisers, brokers, or any other third parties
  • We do not use your data for marketing or targeted advertising

The only exception is if required by law. If a government authority requests your data through a valid legal process, we will comply with applicable law.

6. Google User Data

CRM Lite uses Google Sign-In for authentication. Here's what you should know:

6.1 What Google Provides

When you sign in with Google, Google provides the following information to CRM Lite:

  • Your display name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture URL
  • Your unique Google user ID

6.2 How We Use Google Data

This information is used only to:

  • Authenticate you securely in CRM Lite
  • Display your profile information within the app
  • Manage your user account

6.3 Google's Privacy Practices

CRM Lite does not control Google's practices. Google's collection and use of your data is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy. For information about how Google handles your data, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

6.4 Revoking Access

You can revoke CRM Lite's access to your Google account at any time by:

  • Visiting Google Account Permissions
  • Finding "CRM Lite" in your connected apps and removing access
  • This will prevent you from using CRM Lite, but your customer data on your phone will remain unaffected
