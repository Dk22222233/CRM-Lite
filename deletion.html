Request Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and your right to control your data. Learn how to request deletion of your CRM Lite account and all associated information.

Important Information

  • Local Data: All customer data stored on your phone is not backed up on our servers, so you can delete it directly from your device by uninstalling CRM Lite.
  • Google Account Data: We only store your Google account information (name, email, profile picture, Google ID) used for authentication. You can request deletion of this information.
  • Permanent: Data deletion is permanent. Once deleted, your records cannot be recovered.
  • No Automated System: CRM Lite does not have an automated account deletion system within the app. You must submit a deletion request via email.

What Data CRM Lite Stores

To understand what will be deleted, here's what CRM Lite may store:

1. Local Data (On Your Phone)

All customer data you enter is stored exclusively on your mobile device:

  • Customer names and contact information
  • Transaction history and payment records
  • Outstanding amounts and credit tracking
  • Notes and custom information you add
  • Any app preferences or settings

This data is NOT stored on our servers. You can delete it anytime by uninstalling CRM Lite or deleting individual records within the app.

2. Google Account Information (On Our Systems)

When you sign in with Google, we receive and may store:

  • Your full name (as shown in your Google account)
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile picture URL
  • Your unique Google account ID

This information is used only for authentication. You can request deletion of this authentication data.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Delete Data Locally (On Your Phone)

Your customer records are stored exclusively on your device. You can delete them using one of these methods:

Method A: Uninstall the App

Simply uninstall CRM Lite from your phone to remove all local data:

  1. Long-press the CRM Lite app icon on your home screen
  2. Select "Uninstall" or "Remove"
  3. Confirm the uninstallation
  4. All local customer data will be deleted from your device

Note: This deletes data only from your phone. Your Google authentication data remains in our records (see Option 2 below).

Method B: Delete Records Manually

Delete customer records directly within CRM Lite:

  1. Open CRM Lite and navigate to your customer list
  2. Select a customer record
  3. Look for the delete or remove option
  4. Confirm the deletion
  5. Repeat for each customer you wish to remove

Note: Deleted records cannot be recovered. This does not delete your account or Google authentication data.

Option 2: Request Account and Google Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and Google authentication data from our systems, submit a formal deletion request via email (see instructions below).

Step 1: Prepare Your Request

Send an email to our support team with the following information:

Step 2: Include Required Information

Your deletion request must include the following details:

  • Reason (Optional): You may include a reason for deletion, but it's not required

What Happens After You Submit a Deletion Request

  1. Confirmation: You will receive an email confirming that we received your deletion request.
  2. Verification: We will verify your identity by confirming the email address and name associated with your account.
  3. Processing: Once verified, we will process your deletion request and remove your data from our systems.
  4. Completion: We will send you an email confirming that your data has been deleted.
  5. Timeline: We aim to complete deletion requests within 24-48 hours business days. Some requests may take longer due to verification requirements.

What Gets Deleted

When you request deletion, the following information is removed from CRM Lite's systems:

  • ✓ Your Google authentication account information
  • ✓ Your email address and profile details
  • ✓ Any app preferences or settings associated with your account
  • ✓ Any authentication tokens or session data

What is NOT automatically deleted:

  • ✗ Customer data stored locally on your phone (you must delete this manually or uninstall the app)
  • ✗ Google's records (you would need to contact Google separately)

Additional Actions You Can Take

Revoke CRM Lite Access to Your Google Account

Even without submitting a formal deletion request, you can immediately revoke CRM Lite's access to your Google account:

  1. Visit Google Account Permissions
  2. Find "CRM Lite" in the list of connected apps
  3. Click on CRM Lite and select "Remove access"
  4. Confirm the removal

Result: CRM Lite will no longer have access to your Google account, and you won't be able to sign in to the app. However, this does not delete your authentication data from our systems—submit a formal deletion request for complete data removal.

Uninstall the App

Uninstalling CRM Lite removes all local customer data from your device but does not delete your authentication data from our systems.

Your Right to Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your data at any time. This is guaranteed by:

  • Our Privacy Policy
  • Applicable data protection laws and regulations

We will not charge a fee for processing your deletion request, and we will not require you to justify your reason for deletion.

Deletion FAQ

What's the difference between uninstalling the app and requesting data deletion?

Uninstalling the app removes customer data from your phone but leaves your authentication information in our systems. Requesting data deletion removes both your local data (if you uninstall) and your authentication data from our systems.

Can I recover my data after deletion?

No. Data deletion is permanent. Once deleted from our systems, your information cannot be recovered. Since your customer data is stored locally on your device, make sure to back up important records before deletion if needed.

How long does deletion take?

We aim to process deletion requests within 24-48 hours business days. Verification may take additional time depending on the information provided.

Will you ask for additional information to verify my request?

Yes, we may ask for additional information to verify your identity and ensure we're deleting the correct account. This protects your privacy and security.

Can someone else request deletion of my account?

No. For security reasons, only the account owner can request deletion. We verify the identity of the person making the request before processing.

What if I want to delete only some of my data?

You can delete individual customer records from within the app anytime. To request deletion of only your authentication data (not your account), please specify this in your email request.

Can I re-sign up for CRM Lite after deletion?

Yes. After your account is deleted, you can sign up again using the same or a different Google account. Your previous data will not be recovered.

Does deletion remove my data from Google?

No. We only delete data from CRM Lite's systems. Your Google account and data remain in Google's systems. If you want to delete your Google account or data, you would need to contact Google directly.
