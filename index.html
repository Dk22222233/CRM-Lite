Your Data. Your Phone. Your Control.

✓ Local Storage

All customer data is stored exclusively on your phone using secure local storage. We do not maintain cloud backups of your business data.

CRM Lite uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users securely. We receive only basic Google account information (name, email, profile picture, and unique account ID) needed for authentication.

✓ Privacy First

Your customer records, payment history, and transaction data are never shared with third parties. They remain only on your device.

✓ Full Control

You control your data. You can request deletion of all your information and associated data at any time.
